Estate Maps

Interactive maps of Oracle finance estates: the applications, the business processes they run, and the integrations that connect them.

  • Applications — Oracle Fusion ERP and EPM, analytics, data warehouses and the satellite systems around them, and what each one does.
  • Processes — procure to pay, record to report, close and reporting, with the accounting each step creates.
  • Integrations — every interface by name, with its source, target, lane and cadence.
Internal purposes only. Each map is built from an organisation's own documentation and anonymised. Access is granted only to people who are, or were, part of the organisation a map was sourced from, and maps are not shared with anyone outside it. Registering does not grant access by itself: the administrator reviews every request and assigns the maps you may see.

Workstream

Procure to Pay: eight stages

Zip orchestrates, Oracle decides. The first four stages move a commitment without touching the ledger. Receipt, invoice and payment each create accounting through Subledger Accounting under CORP_GAAP_SLAM. Bank outcomes return hourly and can cancel a payment Oracle already considers made.

Spend category → code combination. Zip proposes segments through three chained lookups. Oracle re-derives the charge account, applies requisition validations and cross-validation rules, and stores the result. A mis-posting has two possible homes: compare what Zip sent with what Oracle stored.
Holds are the Payables workload. 23,722 holds on 15,309 invoices in 12 months; 52% self-clear within an hour; median release 6.3 days, mean 17 days.
No acknowledgement means the bank does not have the payment. Either transmission failed or the file is still in flight. Check Kyriba file status first.
Process

Two paths into the supplier master

PO suppliers are onboarded through Apex and arrive in Oracle through INT055A. Non-PO, ad hoc and Treasury payees are created in Oracle through the Supplier Registration Request and completed by INT959. Both land in the same supplier master.

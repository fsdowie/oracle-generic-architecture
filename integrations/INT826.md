# INT826 — Payment-terms exceptions (Zip → Oracle)

## Purpose
Apply payment-terms exceptions approved in Zip to Oracle transactions, skipping cancelled and paid invoices. `[CO:01 §6, 02 §4]`

## Request log schema (one row per target line) `[DATA:int826-log-analysis/logs]`
`Zip Request Id, Vendor Id, Vendor, Date, Requester Email, Exception Level, Current Exception Level, Current Payment Terms, New Payment Terms, Payment Term Po Number, Payment Term Invoice Number, Invoice Installement Number, Oracle Status, Oracle Status Message, Zip Status, Zip Status Message`

- **Exception Level** (as requested in Zip) and **Current Exception Level** (as resolved): `SUP` (supplier pay site), `PO`, `INV`. A `PO`-level request resolves to invoice-level rows for the invoices on that PO (`PO→INV` is the dominant pattern). `[DATA]` `[INFERRED]`
- `Vendor Id` is the Oracle supplier ID (15-digit, e.g. `<supplier-id>`).
- Success at SUP level returns e.g. `Payment terms updated for supplier pay site US-OAKLA-PO-PAY`.

### User-entry anomalies (Mar–May 2026 error analysis: 41 failed requests) `[CO:INT826_error_analysis.html]`
- Valid PO rule: `PO` + exactly 10 digits (e.g. `PO1100005406`).
- 11 invalid PO formats: REQ numbers (`REQ1100007646`), missing prefix (`3305000198`), malformed (`PO-2210001596`), free text (`i dont have one`).
- 7 notes in invoice field (`INVOICE 26019`, `Invoices for Jan, Feb and March 2026 only`).
- 1 PO number in invoice field; 1 request with 13 invoice numbers in one field.
- 22 of 41 failed requests had no detectable data-entry anomaly → other causes (timing, status, auth).
- Weekly: 14 successful vs 41 error requests (plus 6 other).

## Design implications for an agent
- The dominant failure is **upstream data quality in Zip**, not Oracle. Candidate control: Zip field validation for `^PO\d{10}$` and single invoice number. `[INFERRED]`
- "Not Updated" is mostly **transient** — do not treat as defect unless it persists past the invoice approval/validation cycle.
- 401s indicate service-account/credential problems → OIC `PROD | ERROR | INT826` ticket path.
